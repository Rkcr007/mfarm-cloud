WHAT THIS PAGE CANNOT SEE
Worker heartbeat and host state are not readable from the console. A dead host does not appear here directly — it shows up indirectly, as devices leaving READY without anybody quarantining them.
Keeping this panel is the point. An observability page that implies it sees everything is worse than one that names its blind spot.
