WHAT NEEDS YOU BEFORE DEVELOPMENT STARTS
The IA decision. Four routes refined, or one Fleet surface with lenses. My recommendation and the reasoning are in 03. Only stage 7 of the build order waits on it.
Which console is production. Both are specced, but if it is the React one the component inventory becomes the build list directly.
The five telemetry sources. Each is marked CONFIRM at its point of use, and each has a fallback that is already the design's resting state. Verifying them is what decides how much of bring-up shows progress rather than honest waiting.
Allocator pinning. Everything assumes class-only. If pinning is possible, the picker can promise a unit and the substitution flow is unnecessary.
